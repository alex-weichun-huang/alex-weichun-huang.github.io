Alex Huang

Hi! I'm Alex Huang, a first-year PhD student at the University of Michigan–Ann Arbor, advised by Prof. Emily Mower Provost. My research lies at the intersection of speech-centered machine learning and affective computing, with a focus on developing data-efficient methods for building emotion-aware AI systems.

Before coming to Ann Arbor, I earned my Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from UW–Madison, where I worked with Prof. Yin Li and Prof. Timothy Rogers.
